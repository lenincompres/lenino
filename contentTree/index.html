Recognizing a Wandering House

A wandering house keeps its curtains closed and its front steps pointed toward the nearest horizon.

  • Its chimney leans into the wind
  • Its doorbell answers in unfamiliar languages
  • Its garden contains freshly packed suitcases

Approaching safely

Stand where the welcome mat can see you, then knock exactly twice.

Inviting One to Stay

Offer level ground, good conversation, and a compelling reason to grow roots.

  1. Leave a porch light on
  2. Plant something perennial
  3. Learn the house’s true address
A house settles only after somebody learns its name.
